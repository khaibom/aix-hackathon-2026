# Proposal: Warehouse Visibility Platform for NordFrame Logistics

**Submitted by:** Clarion Data Systems (fictional)
**Variant: MEDIUM — good functional scope, but vague on pricing/timeline/risk**

## Our Understanding
NordFrame's six warehouses currently rely on spreadsheets and a legacy
system, making real-time visibility difficult. We will replace this with a
unified dashboard.

## Proposed Solution
- **Dashboard**: real-time inventory levels across all 6 warehouses, built
  on top of your existing PostgreSQL database — no database migration
  required.
- **Low-stock alerts**: configurable thresholds per item, notifying
  warehouse managers automatically.
- **Role-based access**: warehouse managers see only their own site's data;
  HQ staff have visibility across all sites.
- **Rollout approach**: we will onboard warehouses in phases rather than
  all at once, to reduce disruption during the transition.
